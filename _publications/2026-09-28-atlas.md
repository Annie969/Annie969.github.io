Latent world models plan using the geometry of their representations, but regularizing the overall latent distribution does not preserve the relationships between individual states that action selection depends on. ATLAS transfers normalized pairwise structure from an informative encoder into the planning latent and calibrates the global distribution via Wasserstein embedding matching. Applied to LeWM, it improves goal-reaching success on PushT, TwoRoom, and OGBench-Cube, with the largest gain on higher-novelty TwoRoom episodes.
