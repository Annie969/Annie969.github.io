Large language models often express verbal confidence that is poorly aligned with actual correctness. REHEARSE lets a model adapt from its own scored confidence experience in a credence-calibration game grounded in a strictly proper scoring rule, then applies that cross-instance signal at inference time. Across four LLMs, three benchmarks, and five seeds, it achieves the lowest average ECE among training-free methods.
