Hi~ I am Ke Fang, a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Pennsylvania State University, advised by [Dr. Lu Cheng](https://lcheng.org/). Before that, I received my M.S. from the University of Pennsylvania and my B.E. from Tianjin University.

My research focuses on world models: learning predictive representations of how environments evolve, so that agents can understand, plan, and act. I am broadly interested in the challenges of building world models that are reliable and generalize across tasks. I have also worked on improving the reliability of large language models.

I am always happy to discuss research and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out by email!
